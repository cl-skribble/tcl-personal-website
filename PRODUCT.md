# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Primary: Event organizers, conference programmers, and corporate L&D leads evaluating speakers for keynotes on digital transformation, marketing, and Southeast Asia business growth. They visit to assess credibility, credentials, and fit — quickly.

Secondary: Business partners, investors, and journalists researching Chan Leong's background before a meeting or coverage decision.

## Product Purpose

Establish Teng Chan Leong as a credible, sought-after executive and speaker. The site must communicate authority at first glance — before a single word is read. The primary CTA is booking a speaking engagement.

## Brand Personality

Sharp. Strategic. Credible.

The voice of someone who has built real businesses, earned real recognition, and operates at the intersection of Southeast Asia's old economy and its digital future. Not a thought leader performing depth — an operator who has the receipts.

## Anti-references

- Influencer / personal blog aesthetic: pastel, casual, lifestyle-forward, low institutional weight.
- Stripe-minimal SaaS: restrained blue-on-white, cold, feels like a product page not a person.
- LinkedIn bio dressed up as a site: just a resume with a background — no distinct visual world, no POV.

## Design Principles

1. **Presence before decoration.** The person is the brand. Visual elements serve the human story, not the other way around.
2. **Authority without arrogance.** Credentials are stated, not performed. The design earns trust quietly.
3. **Specificity over vagueness.** Concrete details (KL coordinates, CMU, 10 years in SEA) beat generic superlatives. Every design choice should be motivated, not reflexive.
4. **Editorial, not startup.** The aesthetic register is a Tatler Asia or Time feature, not a SaaS landing page. Art direction over system design.
5. **Earned trust.** Nothing on the page should feel like it's trying too hard. Confidence is quiet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the baseline. Reduced motion respected via `useReducedMotion`. All interactive elements keyboard-reachable. Video backgrounds paired with static fallback. Body text contrast minimum 4.5:1.
